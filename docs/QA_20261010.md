Question import is transactional, validates correct choices and skips repeated questions.

## Academic review still required

This is a prepared question bank, not a claim that every one-mark exercise in every textbook has a fully checked answer. Several books have no printed keys; Tamil text extraction and mathematical layout can require correction. Pending questions are unavailable to student quizzes until an administrator supplies the verified answer and corrects the text. The original textbook remains available for reading.

The SCERT catalog does not identify the edition year for every source PDF. Its currently listed books were synchronized; students and the school must confirm which editions they use. Software tests do not replace subject-teacher review of academic answers or edition confirmation.
